## Day 1 - March 4, 2026

> They say truth stands the test of time. I can think of no greater comfort than knowing this document failed such a test.
>
> - Zampanò, January 5, 1997

### What I've read + inferred:

#### Introduction

- Written by Johnny Truant
- Written before he - presumably - succumbed to the same fate as Zampano, who had died in his apartment, almost fully shut off from the outside world.
- Meant to be a sypnosis of how he had gotten to this point, and warns, promises, who's to say, whoever was reading it that a similar thing will happen to them too
  - No matter how much they dismissed it, it too will come back for them, and it will take away, strip away each and every strand of their assurance, every thing they have taken for granted
  - And eventually, the only thing they will be left with is the comfort of becoming one with the nameless black of the dark
  - And soon, the nightmares will begin.

#### Johnny Truant

- Before that evening, he was looking to find a new place to stay after he had been evicted
- Made ends meet as a tattoo artist, and that led him to be infatuated with the prostitute Thumper
- Greatly regretted having given into Lude's words and fallen into the rabbit hole of the Navidson Records
  - Tried to seek for comfort, warmth through tea, yet his stomach was gone.
- Disregarded Zampanò's words at first, only for them to be the last thing he would forget - if he could forget at all.
- Undefined time revealed that he had spiraled into doing the same things Zampanò did: board up doors, storm-proof everything, shut everything off and drown himself in the darkness
  - An immuntable space.

\=> A victim of his own obsession, dragged away by curiosity and possibly becoming Zampanò's greatest lone audience.

#### Zampanò

- Man hauting the narrative
- Creator (?) of the Navidson Records
- Died at 80 of natural causes (presumably), with his numerous cats either gone or mangled
  - No blunt force trauma, no signs of struggle
- In the dead center of his room was an amalgamation - reams and reams of paper, manuscripts and written napkins; these were the Navidson Records.
- Seemed to have expected others to find it, and subsequently fall into the same spiral he had made himself comfortable in
- Severely depressed, with seven women having left a mark in his heart
  - "There is no greater comfort in my life than those soothing tones cradled in a woman's words."
- He never finished anything, not even the work that consumed his life until the last second.
- Only the sweet embrace of finality - Death herself - was able to rest his heart. Presumably.
- Was "blind as a bat", and yet wrote about sight, wrote about seeing the most
  - What, How we see, and what we CAN'T see
  - His room only goes to elevate his nature: Zero lights, unlit candles, not a single clock anywhere.
    \=> Enigmatic would be a massive understatement to describe Zampanò. In fact, I doubt the very existence of such restrictive labels for him.

#### The Navidson Records

- Little is known about them so far
- Drafted by Zampanò, lodged in the dead center of his room
- Not a single uniformity in design or format
  - the scripts, the line are all over the place
  - each word is scrawled throughout everything, everywhere: napkins, envelopes, scraps of paper,...
- The entire project is about a film that does not exist.
  - Cited books, references, even his inspirations do not exist anywhere in the internet, or even in the history books.
- Claimed to have been made by a Pulitzer Prize-winning photojournalist that must capture the sight of darkness itself.
  \=> The product of passion coughed up by Zampanò, if not - even if driven by madness and obsession.

### Reader's Notes

Holy fuck. I have spent years just yearning to read this book and now that I have finally laid my eyes upon it, I can safely say that this was worth every single cent. Roughly 20 pages of the introduction was enough to hook me into stepping foot onto the Navidson Records, and I'm not willing to look back any time soon.
I'm not ready, but this was not for me to begin with.

## Day 2 - March 6, 2026

> ...All this, don't take it as anything else _but_ this. And if one day you find yourself passing by that <span style="color:rgb(0, 0, 238)">house</span>, don't stop, don't slow down, just keep going. There's nothing there. Beware.
>
> - Will Navidson

### What I've read + inferred:

#### Chapter I

- Introduction of The Navidson Record and Will Navidson himself

#### Will Navidson

- Filmed the Navidson Record, and the prior works
- Seems to have a proper family with siblings and partner
- Claims that the <span style="color:rgb(0, 0, 238)">house</span> is real and urges people against entering it
- Physically and psychologically affected by the <span style="color:rgb(0, 0, 238)">house</span>, shown in the records
- Won the Pulitzer for his picture of a dying girl in Sudan

#### The Five and a Half Minute Hallway

- Dubbed "teaser" for the Navidson Record
- Seemingly impossible geometry dismissed as amateur optical illusions
- While a hot topic, it was never the centre piece in film festivals or official exhibitions

#### Exploration #4

- Dubbed "trailer" for the Navidson Record
- Released after the 5.5 Minute Hallway
- Not a continuous shot, suggesting that these eight minutes were fragments of a larger whole
- Discontinuous, jarring, with jumps all over the place, yet it was enough to convey the supposed tone
- Navidson appeared considerably more perturbed
- New scene of Holloway Roberts, seemingly stalked by something
- While considerably more sought-after than The Five and a Half Minute Halfway, it also sparked heated debates and arguments
- Eventually, interest died out until...

#### The Navidson Record

- Shadowdropped on a limited exposure, yet managed to "unsettle audiances everywhere"
- Established itself as a wonder in media history, with crtics , books, lecture sessions regularly shining their lights on it
- "Hoax of exceptional quality"
- An uneasy combination of... Everything.
  - No matter the genre or catagorization, it always seemed to slip past those boundaries
  - Quite reminiscent of hell in the eyes of its greatest tourist
- Schools of thought are irrelevant, this was meant to be taken **literally**  - so says Will Navidson himself
- Before the Navidson Record there was The Five and a Half Minute Hallway, and after that there was Exploration #4, all of which could be traced back to Will Navidson himself
- Prizes were nominated to it, yet Navidson had never publicly appeared, let alone accept them
- Now, it remains as the country's culture yet no one has truly discovered its nature
- Seemingly never to reach mainstream due to the lack of follow-ups and reveals.

### Reader's Notes

- A lot to think about.
