Naturalism in The Open Boat
\- Man at the mercy of an indifferent natural world
\- Role of individual in community
\- Importance of human relationships

- In The Open Boat
  - Stephen portrays the men on the boat as representatives of human endurance in an indifferent universe
  - The story has a theme of hopelessness contributing to its cynical and morbid view of life
  - The tone and perception of the writing is always somber
  - The four men are always struggling against waves, sharks, their muscles, their acceptance of a nature they don’t control
  - The characters are always hopeless because they are not agents of free will, but puppets to the ocean and the winds and their dinghy
