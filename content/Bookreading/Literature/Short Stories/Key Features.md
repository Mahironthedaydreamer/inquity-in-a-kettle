## Character

- Protagonists - Antagonists
- Minor, static, dynamic characters
- Techniques of Characterization + Judgment
  - Characterization: How the author creates + develops characters
  - Explicit/Implied judgment

## Plot

- Plot devices:
  - Causality: One event leads to another
  - Foreshadowing: Suggestion of what's going to happen
  - Suspense: A sense of worry established by the author (bomb under the table)
  - Conflict: Struggle between opposing forces
- Stages:
  - Exposition -> Rising Action -> Crisis -> Resolution/Denouement

## Structure

- The way a story is organized often provides clues about characters and their actions
- IF the structure is unusual, it can also reflect the author's philosophical + artistic intentions

## Setting

- Place/Location where the story's action happens
- Provides the historical + cultural context that shapes the characters' behavior and choices
- Can also symbolize the emotional state of the characters

## Point of View

- Narrator
- 1st person
- Objective
- Omniscient
  - Editorial
  - Neutral
- Limited omniscience

## Language + Style

- Style: verbal identity of the writer
  - Based on diction/syntax
- Language: A tool to express their tone/attitude toward the subject matter

## Irony

- Verbal irony: Opposite meaning
- Situational irony: Expected event =/= actual outcome
- Dramatic irony: Reader knows more than characters
- Ironic vision: Overall ironic tone of the work
