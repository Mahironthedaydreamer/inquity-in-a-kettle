[[Oscar Wilde]]

- Collection of children's stories written in 1888, dealing primarily with love + selfishness
- Generally sad, with a moralistic message
- Includes:
  - The Happy Prince
  - [[The Nightingale and the Rose]]
  - The Selfish Giant
  - The Devoted Friend
  - The Remarkable Rocket
- No happy diversions for children
  - Appreciated the childlike innocence, but he also realized how often it was abused + disappointed in the adult world
