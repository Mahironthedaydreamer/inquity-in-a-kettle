## 1. Definition

- A brief fictional prose narrative, shorter than novels and usually deals with only a few characters.
- Concerned with a single effect conveyed
- Character is disclosed in action + dramatic encounter
- Usually judged by ability to provide a "complete" treatment of its characters and subject
