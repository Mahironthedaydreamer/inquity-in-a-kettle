## I. Definition

- Invented prose narrative of considerable length + certain complexity that deals with human experience in an imaginative way
- Usually through a sequence of events involving a group of people in a specific setting

## II. [[Classifications]]

- Historical
- Picaresque
- Gothic
- Epistolary
- Sentimental
- Romantic
- Detective
- Fantasy

## III. Main Characteristics of British Novels

### 1. Theme

- Varies from reality, contemporary issues to love and nature

### 2. Plot

- Complex and unpredictable

### 3. Motif

- Nature - Reality, War - Peace, Rural - Urban

### 4. Characters

- Workers, heroes, women, animals, nature

### 5. Settings

- Real-life locale or imaginative world

### 6. Symbol

- The special significance works best when it can fit in a context of naturalism

### 7. Styles

- Romanticism, Realism, Naturalism, Impressionism, Expressionism
