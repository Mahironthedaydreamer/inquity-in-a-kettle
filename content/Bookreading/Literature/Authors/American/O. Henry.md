## I. Life

- Born on September 11, 1862 (during the Civil War) in North Carolina
- At the age of 20, he moved to Texas and started to write under the pen name “O. Henry.”
- In 1896, he was accused of embezzling money from the bank, and 2 years later, he was sent to jail for 5 years
- A heavy drinker. His health deteriorated markedly in 1908, which affected his writing, and he died on June 5, 1910

## II. Writing style

- He always created the special ends described as “tears with smile” for his stories.
- Most stories are set in his own time, the early 20th century, and the background is often employed in New York City.
- His stories showed his sympathy with the lower class.
