I. Challenging Problem / Question
- Not just to remember, but also to use it
II. Sustained Inquiry
- Continuous questioning
	- "What do we know", "What do we need to know"
- Leads to identify investigation and research to be conducted + tasks to be completed, and plan the product
III. Authenticity
- Increases motivation + achievement
- Can be addressed in four different ways
	- Context
	- Tasks
	- Impact
	- Personal
IV. Student Voice + Choice
- "The cognitive act"
- Within boundaries of the project
V. Reflection
- "Metacognition"
- Two-pronged
	- Progress through project tasks + monitor as needed
	- Awareness of learning + problem-solving strategies
- Determines appropriacy of strategies
VI. Critique and Revision
- Essential teacher function + Student activity
- Powerful yet underused
- Formative + Summative
VII. Public Product
- Shares project with audience beyond the classroom
	- More real and consequential than graded schoolwork
	- Motivates others to improve
	- Increase student engagement
