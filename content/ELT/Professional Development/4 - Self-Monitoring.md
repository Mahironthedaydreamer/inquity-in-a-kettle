## I. Nature of self-monitoring

- Starting point in PD is an awareness of what the teacher's current competence + usage of such information as basis for self-appraisal
- Often times this is done by supervisors or managers
  - Provides an outsider's perspective on current level of competence
- However, teachers are also able to make these judgments themselves based on self-collected information
- Systematic approach to the observation, evaluation, management of one's own behavior -> better understanding and control over said behavior
- Three main approaches:
  - Lesson reports
  - Audio-recording a lesson
  - Video-recording a lesson
- To better understand one's teaching + strength/weakness, it's necessary to collect information about teaching behavior and practices with objectivity and under a system and draw out conclusions from there.
- Information usually private, part of the process by which the teacher explores aspects about themself over time
  - However, occasional sharing may prove to be helpful.

## II. Purpose and Benefits of Self-monitoring

- Objective account of one's teaching
  - Realize the gap between subjective perceptions and "objective" reality
  - Notice subconscious habits
- Teacher-initiated
  - Shifts the responsibility for initiating improvements to teachers

## III. Procedures used in self-monitoring

### 1. Lesson reports

- Can be thought as the opposite of a lesson plan
  - Reports what ACTUALLY happened during the lesson
  - Certain aspects can be recalled with high accuracy:
    - Extent of which activities were successful or not
    - Departures from LP
    - Sequence of activities
    - Difficulties with different parts of the lesson
    - Notable aspects
    - Crutch phrases / concepts
  - Focus placed on:
    - What aspects worked well?
    - What aspects did NOT work well? Why?
    - What aspects could be done differently next time?

### 2. Written narrative

- Descriptive summary of the lesson
  - What happened during the lesson?
  - Zero commentary + evaluation
- Teacher critically reviews what happened + comments on what could be improved / learned from the lesson
