## I. What is a Community of Practice?

- A persistent, sustained, **social network** of individual who share and develop an **overlapping knowledge base**, set of beliefs, values, history and experiences focused on a **common practice or mutual enterprise.**
- In other words, it's a **sustainable group of teachers** who develop a **shared knowledge base** focused on a **common goal**.
- Essential Elements of a CoP:
  - Mutual Engagement - A common goal
  - Joint Enterprise - A sustainable group
  - Shared Repertoire - Shared knowledge

## II. What are the benefits of a CoP?

- Develop meaningful and professional relationships in a specific, personalized setting
- Collaborate in a supportive environment
- Share + discover best practices
- Support sustainable changes
- Develop leadership skills + build confidence
- Positive outlook towards professional development

## III. How to develop a CoP?

1. Establish the group
   - From your school
   - From your town
   - From a conference
   - [Ideas - Developing connections in your local networks](https://americanenglish.state.gov/files/ae/resource_files/topic_1_-_f2f_local_networking_final.pdf)
2. Establish the purpose
   - Goals
   - Benefits
3. Identify goals
   - Establish key issues
   - Develop a timeline
     - What to do for each meeting, how frequent should meetings be, etc...
   - Reflect on progress
4. Get organized
   - Logistics:
     - How should these meetings be established, where and how often should they be held?
   - Leadership:
     - Who's going to be leader and for how long?
   - Expectations:
     - What will everyone learn from these sessions?
5. Learn to collaborate
