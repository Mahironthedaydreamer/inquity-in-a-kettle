## I. What is RT?

- Myriad of definitions:
  - Individual practices
  - What a collective group can do
- A teacher can learn about reasons behind their teaching philosophies + practices by asking and answering questions about them
- None superior to another - can always learn strategies from other academic disciplines
- Reflective practice as a fundamental part of continuing PD
  - Provides opportunities to analyze + ask questions about objectives and examine how one plans and teach

## II. How is RT beneficial?

- Teacher who engages in reflective teaching:
  - Identify, analyze and attempt to solve classroom problems
  - Conscious of and questions beliefs about language teaching
  - Cognizant of institutional and cultural contexts in which they teach
  - Responsible for their own PD
- Develop a greater awareness of own teaching + student learning
  - Frees impulsive behavior + monotony in teaching
- Sharing RT experience with other colleagues:
  - More assertive and decisive about personal learning
  - Boost confidence + problem-solving abilities

## III. Struggles and addendums in RT

- Most important is to provide peers and students equal chances of assessment in non-judgmental and supportive ways
- Most difficult is to make a commitment to the chosen methods
