## I. What is Grammar?

- Description of the way in which:
  - Words change forms
  - Words combine into sentences
  - Parts of speech are described.
- When learners master grammar, it means they master these sets of rules and are able to use them for proper self - expression.
- Nine parts of speech:
  - Nouns
  - Verbs
  - Adjectives
  - Adverbs
  - Determiners
  - Prepositions
  - Pronouns
  - Conjunctions
  - Exclamations

## II. What to teach about a grammatical structure?

- 3 dimensions:
  - How is a structure formed?
  - What is expressed through a structure?
  - Why is a structure selected or chosen over others?
- Why teach grammar?
  - Fulfill syllabus requirements
  - Accurate and meaningful communication -> Other skills may be developed in tandem
  - Exam preparation

## III. PPP

### 1. Presentation

- Presenting rules and examples
  - Detailed account on a grammar point + examples: Common approach but not optimal as this alienates learners - asked to memorize without thinking
  - Introduction in a meaningful context will make it more easily understood + remembered
    - Presented in a situation -> grasp meaning + use more easily
    - Chances to assume how it works before they're checked, longer retention.
- Visual presentation
  - Stimulating, clear and direct
    - "too + adjective + to do" -> touch ceiling
  - Not always possible, especially in the case of complex structures
- Presenting through situation
  - Realistic context where it is more likely to occur would be more effective -> Ss can "feel" the grammar point better
  - Better for production later as they can see how it works in real situations
    \=> Use known vocabulary for students to focus on the form + recycle old knowledge for gradual development.

### 2. Practice

- First stage: Controlled practice
  - Usually a drill with the teacher standing in front of a class, works with the whole class and controls what they say
- Second stage: Less controlled practice
  - Usually pair, group work
  - Students work on their own with provided cues
  - Teacher moves from group to group
- 3 reasons:
  - More practice time
  - Can learn from each other without T involvement
  - Encourage quieter students
