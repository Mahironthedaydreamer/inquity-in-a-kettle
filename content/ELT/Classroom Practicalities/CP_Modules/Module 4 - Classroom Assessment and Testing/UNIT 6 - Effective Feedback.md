## I. Overview

### 1. Definition

- Feedback and Feedforward
  - Feedforward: Proactive mechanism that anticipates disturbances -> adjusts system input to prevent errors from occuring
  - Feedback: Reactive mechanism, information from a system's output is used to make corrective adjustments

### 2. Why feedback often fails

- Feedback as telling
- Lack of concrete referents
- Lack of tacit knowledge

## II. Types of feedback

- No one-size-fits-all feedback
- Feedback can be
  - Backward-looking: post-completion
  - Forward-looking: Guiding improvement
- Main types:

1. Corrective Feedback
   - Focuses on alignment with assignement requirements
   - Identifies strength and areas for improvement
2. Epistemic feedback
   - Prompts deeper thinking + clarification
   - Encourages students to explain / justify ideas
3. Suggestive feedback
   - Provides advice / strategies for improvements
   - Points to specific ways to enhance work
4. Epistemic + suggestive feedback
   - Combines reflective questions with concrete suggestions
   - Encourages thinking + action

### III. Characteristics of Effective Feedback

- Goal-referenced
- Consistent
- Tangible + Transparent
- Actionable
- User-friendly
- Timely
- Ongoing
