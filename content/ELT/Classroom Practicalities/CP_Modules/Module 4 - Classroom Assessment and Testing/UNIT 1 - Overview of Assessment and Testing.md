## I. Definitions:

### 1. Broad Sense of Assessment

- A cover term for educational measurement and evaluation in general
  - Educational measurement -> tests, questions, etc.
  - Evalutaion -> Standards

### 2. Narrow Sense of Assessment

- A new paradigm, an alternative to traditional norm-referenced and standardised tests
  - Norm-referenced
  - Standardised
  - Traditional

### 3. Testing

- Specific form of measurement that uses a series of items or an essay -> ascertain where a student is, with regard to some characteristic of interest
- Use of tests, or studies of the theory and practices of their use, development, evaluation, etc.

## II. Is it important? Yes. Why is it important?

- Integral part of teaching - learning process
- Key professional tool for teachers
- Essential component of quality management in teaching and learning
