## I. Definition - What is assessment?

- ubiquitous phenomenom used to regulate cognitive, physical, social, and emotional well-being
- constantly utilized for task completion

## II. Who needs assessment?

- Teachers
- Learners
- School admins
- Parents
- Employers
- Government + Society

### => Multiple stakeholders are served

## III. Usage of Assessment:

- lever for educational reforms (high stakes examinations)
  - university entrance tests
- promote school-based assessment to enhance teaching and learning
  - checking student understanding
  - deciding whether students can advance to the next level or deserve a scholarship
- difference between institutions and teachers/learners
  - policy tool >< **teaching + learning tool** (what, why, how)

## IV. Major Purposes of Assessment:

- should include scoring, ranking, accountability + improvement of teaching and learning
- determine preferences for what is assessed, how it is reported and how it's best interpreted and used

## V. classifications

- achievement - proficiency
  - use assessment to check what we have taught them the past period of time
    - tied to specific materials (textbook, curriculum, teaching, etc)
  - use assessment to check how well they can do something
    - not limited to materials
    - gauge proficiency
    - checking ability instead of what's been taught
- norm-referenced - criterion-referenced
  - difference can be taken as distinction of purpose, more so of interpretation and reporting
  - how people view assessment against norms, criteria
  - norm:
    - rank among students, compare with others, desirable scores, average difficulties, larger sample
  - criteron-referenced
    - set goals achieved, compare with pre-specified levels, scoring irrelevant, dependent outcomes, smaller sample

## VI. Why Understanding Purpose Matters

- Purpose determines Practice
  - WHAT to assess
  - HOW to design
  - HOW to interpret
  - HOW to USE results

## VII. Key Takeways:

### Assessment has MULTIPLE purposes

### Understanding purpose will lead to better assessment design

### Different stakeholders need different information
