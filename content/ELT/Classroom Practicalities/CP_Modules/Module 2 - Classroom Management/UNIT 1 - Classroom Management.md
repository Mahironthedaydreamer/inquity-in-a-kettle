## I.Definition of Classroom Management (CM)

- Refers to all the actions teachers take to create and maintain an environment that supports both academic learning and social-emotional learning
- Strategic, ongoing process
- CM involves organizing physical design of the classroom, establishing rules and instructions, and addressing discipline issues.

## II. Five core components of CM

### a. Physical design of the classroom

- How the classroom space is organized
  - Desk
  - Learning centers
  - Materials
  - Frequently equipment

### b. Rules - Routines

```
SMooth + efficient
Rules: expected behavio
```

### c. Relationships

- Caring, supportive relationships with students, parents
- Encourage positive relationships

### d. Engaging and Motivating Instruction

- Carefully planned lessons with active involvement

### e. Discipline

- Preventing misbehavior + Appropriate response
