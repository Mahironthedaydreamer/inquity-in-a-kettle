## I. Definition of classroom discipline (CD)

- Subset of classroom management
- Methods and strategies teachers use to prevent, address, and correct disruptive student behavior
  \=> Maintaining an environment conducive to learning

## II. Factors contributing to a disciplined classroom

- Positive T-S
- Clear, consistent rules and procedures
- High student engagement
- Teacher awareness (with-it-ness)
- Smooth transitions and routines
- positive reinforcement
- sense of community and shared responsibility

## III. Causes of discipline problems

- Student factors
  - unmet needs
  - social-emotional issues
  - learning differences
  - home environments
- classroom - teacher factors
  - unclear expectations routines
  - poor lesson design
  - ineffective instructions
  - power struggles
  - unfair / inconsistent application of rules
- Environmental factors
  - Physical environment
  - Time of Day

## IV. How to deal with discipline problems

- Key behaviors
  - withitness
    - eyes in back of heads
    - constant monitor
    - preventing many opportunities for misbehavior to occur
  - overlapping
    - ability to do more than 1 thing at a time
    - keep the flow of the lesson going while simultaneously responding to these interruptions
  - signal continuity & momentum
    - teacher's ability to maintain flow
    - moves along at a good pace: not too fast or too slow
    - teacher avoids pauses in the lesson
      \=> T makes sure they are well-prepared & all the necessary materials for every lesson are organized before the day
- 2 types of misbehavior
  - minor
    - calling out, daydreaming, talking to classmate instead of
  - more serious
    - fighting, bullying, disrespect
- key guidelines to address misbehavior
  - preserve dignity
  - keep instructional program going with minimal disruption
  - culturally consistent language
