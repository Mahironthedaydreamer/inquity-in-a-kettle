## II. Course Path

- Week 1 + Week 2: Coursebook evaluation
  - Week 1:
    - Criteria involved in choosing coursebook materials
    - Analysis of different textbooks
  - Week 2:
    - Coursebook use and adaptation
    - Micro-teaching: Students teach a lesson, adapting the textbook when necessary
- Week 3: Classroom management
- Week 4-6: Formative and summative assessment
  - Week 4: Overview of testing and assessment
    - Purposes of assessment
    - Different assessments
    - Assessment for learning and assessment of learning
  - Week 5: Assessment for learning
    - Effective questioning techniques
    - Characteristics of effective feedback
    - Micro-teaching
  - Week 6: Assessment of learning
    - Different types of tests
    - CEFR descriptions
- Week 7-8: Test evaluation
  - Week 7:
    - Principles of test item design
    - Sample test analysis
  - Week 8:
    - Practice: test item design (focus on language components)
- Week 9-10: Assessing reading + listening skills
  - Skills / tasks / activities
  - Practice: listening/ reading item designs
- Week 11(-12): Assessing speaking
  - Speaking assessment criteria
  - Practice: Speaking skill assessment
  - Micro-teaching: Teachers organize a speaking activity and assess students' speaking performances.
- Week 13(-14): Assessing writing
  - Writing assessment criteria
  - Practice: writing skill assessment
- Week 15: Revision
